Cryptocurrency Risk Disclosure

[Placeholder — to be completed by qualified legal counsel before any live offering]

Digital assets such as Bitcoin and Ethereum are highly speculative and volatile. Their value can decrease to zero. They are not legal tender in most jurisdictions and are not backed by any government or central bank.

Key risks include extreme price volatility, regulatory uncertainty, cybersecurity threats, operational failures of exchanges or custodians, irreversible transactions, and lack of traditional investor protections (such as deposit insurance).

Only invest amounts you can afford to lose entirely. This document does not cover all risks. Seek independent advice.
