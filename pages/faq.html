Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a real investment platform?▼

This is a frontend prototype for demonstration. It does not currently offer live investment services. Regulatory status and service availability would be verified before any launch.

Are returns guaranteed?▼

No. No investment return is guaranteed. You may lose some or all of your capital.

What assets can I access?▼

The prototype presents digital assets (e.g. BTC, ETH) and technology equities (including TSLA) for illustration. Actual offerings depend on regulation and product design.

How are fees structured?▼

See the Fees page. Figures shown are prototype examples and must be replaced with actual fees before launch.

Is cryptocurrency safe?▼

Cryptocurrency is highly volatile and carries significant risk of loss, including total loss. See the Cryptocurrency Risk Disclosure.

How do I contact support?▼

Visit the Support page for ticket, chat (placeholder), and contact form options. Hours and details are editable placeholders.
