Digital Assets

Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), and other eligible digital assets

Higher Volatility

Digital assets are decentralized digital representations of value that may be used as a medium of exchange or store of value. Prices can move rapidly in either direction based on adoption, regulation, technology developments, and market sentiment.

Potential benefits

  • Exposure to a growing asset class
  • Potential for long-term appreciation (not guaranteed)
  • 24/7 market accessibility in many jurisdictions

Key risks

  • Extreme price volatility; possibility of total loss
  • Regulatory uncertainty and potential restrictions
  • Custody, cybersecurity, and operational risks
  • Liquidity risk during stressed markets
Suggested horizon: Medium to long term (3+ years)

Technology Equities

Publicly traded technology companies, including Tesla (TSLA)

Market Risk

Equity investments represent ownership stakes in companies. Technology sector stocks can offer growth potential but are subject to company-specific news, competitive dynamics, valuation changes, and overall equity market conditions.

Potential benefits

  • Participation in innovation-driven companies
  • Liquidity via public markets (subject to market hours)
  • Potential capital appreciation and, in some cases, dividends

Key risks

  • Share prices can decline significantly
  • Company-specific and sector concentration risk
  • Macroeconomic and interest-rate sensitivity
  • No guarantee of positive returns
Suggested horizon: Medium to long term (3–7+ years)

Diversified Strategies

Combinations of eligible assets based on risk profile

Profile-Based

Diversified strategies allocate across digital assets, technology equities, and cash or cash equivalents according to a selected risk profile. Diversification aims to reduce concentration risk but does not eliminate the possibility of loss.

Potential benefits

  • Broader exposure across asset types
  • Alignment with stated risk tolerance
  • Structured rebalancing framework (where applicable)

Key risks

  • All underlying asset risks still apply
  • Correlation can rise in stressed markets
  • Fees may reduce net returns
  • No strategy guarantees capital preservation or gains
Suggested horizon: Matches strategy (typically 3+ years)
